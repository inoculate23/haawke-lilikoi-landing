The Book · Working Title
Which Do You Prefer?
A Memoir of Extraordinary Minds and the Intelligence That Was Always Coming
For thirty years Craig Ellenwood kept ending up in the wrong rooms at the right time. He co-founded the music element of Burning Man on a long shot pitch to Larry Harvey. He put on a VR helmet at a cyberarts conference in Pasadena and took it off to find Timothy Leary's face inches from his, grinning. He ate unidentified capsules from a bowl at a house party and found himself in a Turkish bath discussing the nature of reality with Nick Herbert. He performed with Psychic TV. He sat with Robert Anton Wilson in an airport and felt like he'd known him forever. On the 50th anniversary of the discovery of LSD he called Albert Hofmann — who was there on the original bicycle — and asked him the same question Leary had asked Craig years before.
He never understood why he kept ending up in these rooms until an AI told him.
