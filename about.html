Our Journey

From analog roots to neural frontiers: The evolution of Haawke.

  • 1991
    Debut of Spatial Audio

    We introduced our 3D spatial audio system in San Francisco on New Year's Eve at The Design Center, Toontown. We blew some minds.

    View Flyer
  • 1992
    Tours & Releases

    Toured with Moby, Psychic TV, and more. Released four audio CDs on Silent Records.

  • 1992
    Burning Man Origins

    Founded the music element of Burning Man as the first sound camp and DJs.

    Read: Burning Man's First Sound Camp
  • 1993
    Global Rhythms

    Lived in India for six months making music with trancemasters from around the world.

  • 1996
    Visual FX Roots

    Interned at San Francisco DV Garage under Alex Lindsey (Ex-ILM Rebel Mac Unit), learning commercial 3D and motion graphics techniques.

  • 2003
    12 Years in Paradise

    Owned and operated Craig Ellenwood Photography in Honolulu, Hawaii (2003-2015).

    View Portfolio
  • 2015
    Inoculate Media

    Launched Inoculate.Media in Vancouver, BC. Built video pipelines for TV/Film and began exploring AI/ML.

    Visit Inoculate.Media
  • 2022
    Las Vegas & Area 15

    Relocated to Las Vegas. Created large-scale projection mapping and immersive environments at Area 15.

  • 2023
    Haawke Neural Technology

    Launched Haawke Neural Technology to bring AI/ML applications to large-scale events and performances.

    Supported by Google's TPU Research Cloud (TRC).
  • 2025
    Lilikoi Launch

    Launched Lilikoi, the world's first AI-integrated production-grade A/V software.
