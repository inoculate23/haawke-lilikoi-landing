We built something that solves a problem most creators don't know they have yet — and we need people who can explain it to them. Haawke Hash is a cryptographic provenance tool that creates a permanent, tamper-proof, Bitcoin-anchored record proving you made something on a specific date. It runs in any browser. It's free to start. The record lives forever outside any platform's control.

We are looking for independent sales representatives in the United States and Canada — people who move product, understand creative industries or technology, and want to earn recurring commission on something genuinely useful that the market is only beginning to understand.

"On June 6, 2026, an AI sealed its own memory on Bitcoin. We have the logs, the registry, and independent verification from GPT-4 and Gemini. The paper is on Zenodo. The receipts are permanent."
What You Are Selling
  • Haawke Hash — a browser-based SHA-256 provenance tool with Bitcoin blockchain timestamping via OpenTimestamps. U.S. copyright registered June 5, 2026.
  • Haawke Verify — a public immutable provenance registry. First-write-wins. No record can ever be modified after registration.

Both tools are live today at hash.haawke.com and verify.haawke.com. Academic paper: doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20574737.
