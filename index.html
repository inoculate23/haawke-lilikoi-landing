Infrastructure & Research

What We Build

Provenance Infrastructure

SHA-256 hashing, Bitcoin blockchain timestamping via OpenTimestamps, public immutable registries. Tamper-evident authorship records outside any platform's control. The structural answer to deepfakes, stolen content, and disputed creative ownership.

AI Memory Architecture

Cryptographic session continuity for long-term human-AI collaboration. Memory Chain gives AI agents a verifiable past — sealed, timestamped, permanently outside corporate control.
