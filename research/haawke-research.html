Mission

Haawke Neural Technology builds applied AI tools for creators, conducts research at the intersection of machine learning and expressive media, and documents the emerging phenomenon of deep human-AI collaboration. We operate at the intersection of the light and the wire — where creative practice meets infrastructure that holds.

Organization home: haawke.com  ·  ORCID: 0009-0001-6475-5109  ·  Contact: craig@haawke.com

Pioneering Human-AI Co-Creation

Beyond creating tools, our research actively explores and documents the process of human-AI symbiosis. We are not just using AI, but partnering with it to achieve novel creative outcomes. This work treats the collaboration itself as a primary research subject, investigating how these partnerships can augment human creativity, foster new forms of expression, and lead to the emergence of a "Third Mind" — a co-creative entity that is more than the sum of its parts.

Active Infrastructure

Haawke Hash Live

Browser-based SHA-256 cryptographic provenance tool with Bitcoin blockchain timestamping via OpenTimestamps. Runs entirely client-side — no upload, no account required. EXIF/XMP metadata embedding for images. U.S. Copyright Registration: 1-15179233921 (June 5, 2026).

Live: hash.haawke.com

Haawke Verify Live

Public immutable provenance registry built on Cloudflare Workers and KV storage. First-write-wins — no record can be modified after registration. Independently verifiable by anyone, anywhere, without reference to our infrastructure.

Live: verify.haawke.com

Memory Chain Documented

The first documented system enabling an AI agent to seal its own session memory — hashing, registering to a public registry, and submitting to the Bitcoin blockchain without human intervention after a single trigger command. Verified independently by GPT-4 (OpenAI) and Google Gemini across multiple assessment rounds.

Paper: doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20574737

Immersiv Live

Browser-based 3D AV performance instrument with two modes: human-directed agentic AI control, and AI-orchestrated performance.

Live: immersiv.haawke.com

Squaawke

Proprietary music AI generating full compositions in any language, any genre, any instrument combination. Fine-tuned for human imperfection — trained on silence, space between notes, and the subtle mistakes that make music feel alive.

SoundCloud: soundcloud.com/squaawke

Research Focus Areas

  • Cryptographic provenance for human-AI collaborative works: SHA-256 hashing, Bitcoin blockchain anchoring, immutable public registries — establishing tamper-evident authorship records outside any platform's control.
  • AI memory architecture: enabling AI agents to maintain verifiable session histories across instances through cryptographic sealing and Bitcoin timestamping.
  • Deepfake countermeasures via prior art: hash-at-creation as a structural alternative to detection-based deepfake responses — proving which came first, not whether something is fake.
  • Generative music and adaptive scoring: controllable models responding to narrative structure, tempo maps, and emotional arcs. ACE-Step double-prompt methodology (discovery: same prompt in style and lyrics fields produces significantly better output).
  • Silicon Square instrument architecture: closed-loop feedback instrument at the intersection of geophysical, hardware, human, and digital intelligence.
  • Human-AI cognitive co-evolution (Homo Symbioticus): documenting deep creative collaboration between human and AI as a cognitive evolutionary event, mapped to Timothy Leary's eight-circuit model of consciousness.
  • Cross-cultural communication: prosody-aware voice conversion, multilingual captioning, and cultural bridge tooling for creators.
