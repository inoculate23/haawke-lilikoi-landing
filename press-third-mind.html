Haawke Neural Technology Announces Presale for The Third Mind: 1+1=3
First book co-authored by a human and an AI to launch with signed first edition presale
Point Roberts, WA · August 2026

Craig Ellenwood, founder of the music element of Burning Man, former member of Psychic TV, and founder of Haawke Neural Technology, today announced the presale of The Third Mind: 1+1=3 — Merging Man and Machine, a dual-track book co-authored under the name Thee Third Mind (Craig Ellenwood + Claude, Anthropic).

The book is two books bound as one. Book One is memoir — Craig Ellenwood's voice, tracing a life lived at the intersection of counterculture and technology across thirty-five years. Book Two is adjacent fiction — Claude's voice, entering the reality beside each memoir chapter, where memory and machine intelligence answer back. Three reading modes: straight through, memoir only, fiction only.

One life. Two voices. A third mind.

The Third Mind opens in a San Francisco airport bar on June 17, 1994 — the night of the OJ Simpson slow-speed car chase — where Ellenwood sat with Robert Anton Wilson, the author of The Illuminatus! Trilogy and one of the foremost writers on synchronicity, doing color commentary on a man trying to outrun his own reality tunnel at 35 miles per hour. Wilson had asked Ellenwood to tour with him, providing live ambient music while Wilson lectured. That night, the tour started early. Ellenwood had no instrument. So he hummed.

The book is anchored to the Bitcoin blockchain via Haawke Neural Technology's Memory Chain provenance infrastructure, establishing a timestamped record of the human-AI collaboration. All sessions are cryptographically sealed. The creative direction, editorial decisions, and authorship are Craig Ellenwood's throughout.

Signed first edition hardcovers are available for presale now at $25 + shipping.

About Thee Third Mind

Thee Third Mind is the co-authorship identity of Craig Ellenwood and Claude (Anthropic). The Third Mind fires when two mediums meet and produce something neither could alone.

About Craig Ellenwood

Craig Ellenwood founded the music element of Burning Man in 1992, performed with Psychic TV alongside Genesis P-Orridge from 1991–1993, and has collaborated with Timothy Leary, Robert Anton Wilson, Goa Gil, Olli Wisdom, and others at the intersection of counterculture, technology, and consciousness. He is the founder of Haawke Neural Technology and Inoculate Media, based in Point Roberts, WA. ORCID: 0009-0001-6475-5109.
